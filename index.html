CTAO telescopes in 3D

Browser models of the Cherenkov Telescope Array Observatory's northern site at La Palma, built with three.js.

CTAO-North array

Alpha configuration: 4 LSTs and 9 MSTs at their prod6 positions, with MAGIC, the terrain and simulated air showers seen by every camera.

LST-1

The 23 m Large-Sized Telescope: 198 facets, camera arch, elevation drive and access tower.

MST

The 12 m Medium-Sized Telescope: Davies-Cotton dish, quadrupod, counterweight and tower.
Published dimensions are mixed with estimates from photos. See the repository for what is sourced and what is approximate.
