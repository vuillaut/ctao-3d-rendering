CTAO telescopes in 3D

3D models of the Cherenkov Telescope Array Observatory's telescopes and arrays. This site is for educational and demonstration purposes, the physics of the showers and telescopes has been simplified.

CTAO-North array

Alpha configuration: 4 LSTs and 9 MSTs, with MAGIC, the terrain and simulated air showers seen by every camera.

CTAO-South array

Alpha configuration near Paranal, Chile: 14 MSTs and 37 SSTs in the Atacama Desert, with simulated air showers seen by every camera.

LST

The 23 m Large-Sized Telescope: 198 facets, camera arch, elevation drive and access tower.

MST

The 12 m Medium-Sized Telescope: Davies-Cotton dish, quadrupod, counterweight and tower.

SST

The 4.3 m Small-Sized Telescope: dual-mirror optics, 2048-pixel SiPM camera, fork mount and counterweights.
